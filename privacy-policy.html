Privacy Policy

This policy explains how BibleWise handles information when you use the BibleWise mobile application.

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Publication checklist: This is a working draft.

1. Who is responsible for BibleWise?

BibleWise is published and maintained by sleyDEV (“BibleWise,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). For privacy questions, contact support@biblewise.app.

2. Information handled by the app

BibleWise does not require you to create an account to use its core features. Depending on how you use the app, the following information may be processed:

  • Questions and conversation content: The questions you submit and relevant conversation or Scripture context may be transmitted to OpenAI to generate an answer.
  • Scripture lookup information: When the app requests Bible content, relevant Bible references, search terms, language or locale information, and translation identifiers may be sent to Biblia or its service endpoints to retrieve Scripture content.
  • Information stored on your device: The app stores app data locally, which may include chat history, saved favorites, preferences such as language and appearance, and cached Bible chapters.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in questions or messages unless it is necessary for your request.

3. Why this information is used

The information described above is used to provide Bible question-and-answer functionality, retrieve requested Scripture passages, display your saved history and favorites, remember your app preferences, and improve the reliability and operation of the app.

4. Service providers

BibleWise relies on third-party services for some features:

  • OpenAI: Receives submitted prompts and relevant context so its AI service can generate responses. OpenAI processes information under its own terms and privacy documentation: OpenAI Privacy Policy.
  • Biblia: Provides Bible text retrieval services. Requests may include the Scripture reference or search information and the selected language or translation. See Biblia for information about its service.

Third-party providers process information under their own policies and terms. Their handling and retention practices are not controlled by this policy.

5. Local storage and deletion

Chat history, favorites, preferences, and cached Bible content may be stored locally on your device. You can use the relevant controls provided by the app to manage or delete locally stored items where those controls are available. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored app data, but device backups or operating-system behavior may affect what is retained.

Information sent to a third-party provider is subject to that provider's systems and retention policies. Deleting information inside BibleWise does not necessarily delete copies already processed by a third-party provider.

6. Sharing and sale of information

BibleWise does not sell your personal information. Information is shared with the service providers described in this policy when needed to provide the app's features. This statement does not cover information those providers process independently under their own policies.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the app's design to protect information. No method of electronic transmission or storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children

BibleWise is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so we can review the concern.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

For questions or requests relating to this Privacy Policy, contact support@biblewise.app.
