## Before publishing
1. Replace `[INSERT THE LEGAL OWNER OR RESPONSIBLE PUBLISHER NAME]` in both legal pages.
2. Replace `[INSERT THE JURISDICTION TO BE USED]` in the Terms & Conditions.
3. Verify every privacy statement against the current production app and provider configuration.
4. Confirm the contact email is monitored.
5. Confirm rights and attribution requirements for the Bible translations used by the app.
6. Update the effective/last-updated dates when the documents are approved.

Do not submit the Privacy Policy URL to Google Play until the policy has been reviewed and all placeholders have been resolved.
